Pepers is an open-source Android application for managing tailoring-shop accounts and daily work.

Features include:
- Recording tailoring work and production.
- Individual and numeric registration modes.
- Managing piece names and prices.
- Recording expenses.
- Organizing records by days, months and years.
- Reports and PDF export and sharing.
- Separate shop accounts and local data management.
- Optional account login and cloud synchronization.
- Arabic RTL user interface.

The application is designed to keep shop accounting organized while remaining usable offline. When an account and synchronization are enabled, data can be synchronized when an internet connection is available.
